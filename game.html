Last Suit Standing
A perfect-play hand
Offer · Choose · ScoreUnchosen cards stay in the row

Rules in 45 seconds
  1. Offer two neighboring cards. Your opponent takes one and gains its value in chips.
  2. You gain the other card’s value in chips. That card stays in the row; close the gap.
  3. The last card sets trump and pays its value once more to the last offerer.
  4. The last chooser swaps first. Each player may exchange one stack card; chips never move.
  5. Stacks score again: trump ×2, pairs ×2, both ×4. Highest total wins.

Keyboard: ← → to step · Space to play or pause
